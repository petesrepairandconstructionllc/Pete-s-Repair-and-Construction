About Pete's Repair

Your Trusted Local Repair & Remodeling Partner

At Pete’s Repair and Construction, we offer a complete, one-stop solution for all your home maintenance, repairs, and upgrade requirements. Based in Gene Autry, OK, we combine decades of practical hands-on experience with an unyielding commitment to excellence.

Our technicians execute every job with meticulous planning, transparent pricing, and respect for your home. From minor fixes to structural adjustments, we aim for maximum comfort, security, and beauty.
